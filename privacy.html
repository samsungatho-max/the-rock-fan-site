Privacy policy

Who we are

. Personal data sent through it is handled by (“we”).

What we collect

When you use the contact form we receive what you enter: your name, e-mail address, country, the subject, type and text of your request and, if you choose to give it, your phone number. The website sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Why, and on what basis

We use these details only to read and answer your request. The legal basis is your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by asking us to delete your request.

Who can see it

Only the site administrators. Requests are stored with our hosting providers (Vercel and Upstash) and a notification is sent to the administrators through an e-mail delivery service (Resend). They process the data on our behalf, and may do so outside your country. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else, and they are not passed on to Dwayne Johnson or his representatives.

How long we keep it

Requests are deleted once they have been dealt with, and at the latest twelve months after they were sent.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent us, or object to their use, by sending a new request through the contact form. You may also complain to the data-protection authority of your country.
